One to one tutoring is more effective than online tutoring because of the strong focus on communication and improved concentration. One to one tutoring improves learning in a better way.

It has many advantages which cannot be enjoyed by kids participating in online classes, because the student and tutor are there in the same room face to face, which provides better personal interaction between student and tutor and students can ask questions whenever they need it.

## One to One Tutoring Helps With Student's Weaknesses.

One to one tutoring helps students strengthen their weaknesses. In this case, it is important for the parents to identify the weak areas of their child and then determine how they can be improved. If you notice that your child has problems with math or reading, then you can hire a tutor who specialises in these subjects. This way, they will be able to focus on improving their weak areas and improve their grades in school.

## One to One Tutoring Improves A Child's Grades.

One to one tutoring is the best choice for a child who needs extra help with their grades, so that they can get them up to the level of other students in their grade level.

The one on one attention from their tutor will help them to understand how to study more effectively and how to retain information better. In addition, this type of tutoring will give your child the chance to ask questions about things that are confusing or difficult for them.

## One to One Tutoring Encourages Student's To Take More Initiative.

One to one tutoring encourages student's to take more initiative. It is easier for your student to ask questions and get help when they don't feel like they are holding up the class or taking too much time away from other students.

With one to one tutoring, they can learn at their own pace, which encourages them to take more initiative in their learning.

## One to One Tutoring Offers Better Value Than Online Tutoring

Online tutoring has grown in popularity as an affordable option for parents who want to help their children with schoolwork. However, in some cases one to one tutoring can be less expensive than online tutoring.

This is because the cost of online tutoring is often based on the number of hours you use and not the specific needs of your child or family. If your child struggles with writing, for example, but does well in other subjects, then a typical online solution may not be ideal. One to one tutoring allows you to have unlimited access to a tutor who will work on whatever your child needs most at any time during the year.
