TutorMunk delivers face to face tutoring for primary students, high school students and senior students following the NSW NESA syllabus. We do not deliver online or remote tutoring. Sessions are delivered in four ways: one to one in the family home, small group in the family home, one to one at our Gregory Hills centre, and small group at our Gregory Hills centre. Group sessions at the centre are capped at six students in the same stage. Tutors are engaged as independent contractors.

## Enrolment and Eligibility

### Who May Enrol

Only a parent or legal guardian aged 18 or over may enrol a student. A student under 18 cannot enrol themselves. By enrolling you confirm that you are the parent or legal guardian, that you may consent on the student's behalf, and that the information you give us is true and complete.

### Information You Must Provide

You must give us accurate contact details, the residential address where in-home sessions will take place, any access information the tutor needs, the names of any other adults authorised to be present or to collect the student, and any learning, medical or behavioural information relevant to delivering sessions safely.

### Changes

You must tell us promptly if your address, contact details, authorised adults or your child's needs change.

## Scheduling and Delivery

### Weekly Slots

Sessions are normally scheduled as a fixed weekly time with the same tutor, so the student builds a working relationship with one person. Changes to the weekly time are subject to tutor availability.

### In-Home Sessions

For every in-home session a responsible adult must be present in the home for the entire session. The session must take place in an open, accessible area of the home, such as a dining room, kitchen or lounge room. Sessions must not take place in a bedroom. The adult present signs the session record at the end of the session. If no responsible adult is present, the tutor will not commence the session, and the session is treated as a cancellation with less than 24 hours notice.

A student aged 16 or over may be tutored without an adult present only where the parent or guardian has given written permission in advance.

### Centre Sessions

Sessions at our Gregory Hills centre take place in rooms that are visible from outside or with the door open. Students must be signed in and signed out. A student will only be released to a parent, guardian or another adult you have authorised in writing.

### What You Need to Provide

For in-home sessions you must provide a suitable, quiet and reasonably tidy space with a table and adequate lighting, free from hazards, with any dogs or other animals secured away from the working area, and safe access and parking for the tutor. You must not smoke or vape in the room during the session.

### Punctuality

If a student is not ready at the agreed start time, the session still ends at the scheduled finish time. The tutor is not required to wait more than 15 minutes, after which the session may be treated as a no-show.

## Cancellations and Refunds

Full terms are set out in the TutorMunk Cancellation and Refund Policy. In summary, a session cancelled with at least 24 hours notice is rescheduled at no charge subject to tutor availability. A session cancelled with less than 24 hours notice, or a no-show, is forfeited and charged in full, because the tutor has already committed the time and in most cases has travelled. Where TutorMunk cancels a session, it is rescheduled at no charge or refunded. Where there is any inconsistency, the Cancellation and Refund Policy applies.

## Child Safety

TutorMunk has zero tolerance for child abuse, neglect and exploitation. We are committed to the safety, participation and wellbeing of every child we work with, and we align our practices with the NSW Child Safe Standards and the National Principles for Child Safe Organisations.

- Every tutor holds a current Working with Children Check, verified by us before their first session and monitored for expiry.
- Every tutor is bound by the TutorMunk Tutor Code of Conduct, which prohibits private contact with students outside sessions, private tutoring arranged directly with a family, transporting a student in a vehicle, and any conduct inconsistent with child safety.
- An adult must be present for in-home sessions, and centre sessions are conducted in visible rooms.
- Any concern about a child's safety must be reported to us immediately at hello@tutormunk.com.au or on 1300 00MUNK (1300 006 865). Where there is immediate danger, call 000.

We escalate concerns to the NSW Police Force and to child protection authorities where the law requires it or where we consider escalation appropriate. Anyone may raise a concern without fear of reprisal. Our full approach is set out in the TutorMunk Child Safe Policy.
