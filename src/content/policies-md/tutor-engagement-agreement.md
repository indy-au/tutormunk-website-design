## Parties

This agreement is made between TutorMunk (ABN: TBC, ACN: TBC) of Level 27, International Towers, 100 Barangaroo Avenue, Sydney NSW 2000, and the individual tutor named in the engagement schedule. The tutor is engaged as an independent contractor and not as an employee, partner or agent of TutorMunk.

## Nature of the Engagement

### Independent Contractor

The tutor is responsible for their own taxation, superannuation, insurance and any other obligation arising from their business. Nothing in this agreement creates an employment, partnership or agency relationship.

### Non-Exclusive

This agreement is non-exclusive. The tutor may work for other clients, provided they do not breach the Code of Conduct, the confidentiality clause or the non-solicitation clause.

### Services

The tutor will deliver face to face tutoring to TutorMunk students, in the family home or at the TutorMunk centre at Gregory Hills, aligned to the NSW NESA syllabus, and will record a short written note after every session. TutorMunk does not offer online tutoring and the tutor must not deliver a TutorMunk session online without our written agreement.

## Eligibility and Compliance

### Mandatory Requirements

The tutor represents and warrants that throughout the engagement they hold:

- a current Working with Children Check issued in New South Wales, in the paid worker category;
- a current Australian driver licence, and access to a safe and roadworthy vehicle where they attend homes;
- an active Australian Business Number;
- a qualification or study relevant to the subjects and stages they teach.

The tutor must provide evidence of each on request. TutorMunk verifies the Working with Children Check before the first session and records the number, the expiry date and the verification outcome. The tutor must tell TutorMunk immediately if any of these lapses, is suspended, is revoked, or becomes the subject of an investigation. A tutor whose Working with Children Check is expired, barred or interim barred will be removed from all work with children immediately.

### Subject and Stage Match

The tutor may only deliver sessions in the subjects and stages approved by TutorMunk.

### Insurance

The tutor is responsible for their own public liability insurance, professional indemnity insurance and any vehicle insurance required for travel to sessions. TutorMunk may require evidence of cover.

## Rate, Invoicing and Payment

### Rate

The tutor's rate is set out in the engagement schedule and is specific to that tutor. Rates include GST where the tutor is registered for GST. Rates are confidential.

### Invoicing

Invoicing runs on a fortnightly cycle. The tutor invoices TutorMunk on the Friday of week two for all sessions delivered in weeks one and two. TutorMunk pays by electronic funds transfer on the Friday of week three to the account nominated by the tutor.

### Invoice Requirements

Each invoice must show the tutor's Australian Business Number, the invoice number and date, the period covered, a line for each session with the date, subject, student identifier, duration and rate, GST where applicable, and the total payable.

### Travel

Rates for in-home sessions take account of reasonable local travel within the agreed service area. Travel outside that area is agreed in advance in writing.

### Late Cancellation and No-Show by the Family

Where a family cancels with less than 24 hours notice or does not attend, and TutorMunk charges the family, the tutor is paid 50 per cent of their rate for that session. Where the tutor had already travelled to the home before the cancellation, the tutor is paid 100 per cent of their rate. Where the family is not charged, no cancellation payment is due.

### Tutor No-Show

Failing to attend a scheduled session without notice is a serious matter. Two tutor no-shows will end this agreement.

### No Minimum Hours

TutorMunk does not guarantee any minimum number of sessions or hours.

## Scheduling and Availability

### Weekly Slots

Tutors are assigned a fixed weekly time per student. The tutor confirms availability at the start of each term and gives at least 7 days notice of any change where possible.

### Rescheduling

If the tutor needs to reschedule, they must give TutorMunk at least 24 hours notice. TutorMunk coordinates with the family. The tutor must not arrange a change directly with the family.

### Illness and Emergencies

If the tutor cannot attend because of illness or an emergency, they must tell TutorMunk as early as possible by phone on 1300 00MUNK (1300 006 865) and by email to hello@tutormunk.com.au. The tutor must not attend a session while unwell with a contagious illness.

### Arriving at a Home

The tutor must arrive on time, must not arrive early without agreement, and must not enter the home unless a responsible adult is present. Detailed requirements are in the TutorMunk In-Home and Centre Safety Policy, which forms part of this agreement.

## Delivery Standards

- Attend on time, prepared, with the materials needed for the session.
- Deliver the session in an open, accessible area of the home, never a bedroom, with a responsible adult present throughout, and have that adult sign the session record at the end.
- At the centre, work in a room that is visible from outside or with the door open.
- Align content to the NSW NESA syllabus for the student's stage and subject.
- Write a short session note covering what was taught and what to practise, and submit it so the family receives it within 3 business days.
- Contribute to a fuller report each term.
- Treat students, families and TutorMunk staff with courtesy and respect at all times.
- Raise any concern, incident or complaint with TutorMunk promptly.

## Code of Conduct

The tutor is bound by the TutorMunk Tutor Code of Conduct, which is a separate document provided with this agreement and forms part of it. The tutor signs the Code of Conduct separately. A breach of the Code is a material breach of this agreement and may result in immediate termination.

Without limiting the Code, the tutor must not contact a student outside a TutorMunk session, arrange private tutoring with a TutorMunk family, meet a student anywhere other than a scheduled session, transport a student in a vehicle, or be alone with a student behind a closed door.

## Child Safety and Reporting

TutorMunk has zero tolerance for child abuse, neglect and exploitation. The tutor commits to the safety and wellbeing of every child they work with and to practise consistently with the NSW Child Safe Standards.

The tutor must report any concern about a child's safety to TutorMunk immediately, by email to hello@tutormunk.com.au and, in an urgent case, by phone on 1300 00MUNK (1300 006 865). Where a child is in immediate danger the tutor must call 000 first. The tutor must cooperate fully with any TutorMunk or statutory investigation. Reporting a concern in good faith will never disadvantage a tutor.

The tutor acknowledges that a person who delivers education services to children in the course of paid work may be a mandatory reporter under the Children and Young Persons (Care and Protection) Act 1998. TutorMunk requires every tutor to act as though this obligation applies to them.

## Performance

TutorMunk reviews tutor performance regularly against attendance and punctuality, timeliness and quality of session notes, family and student feedback, student engagement, and currency of compliance documents. Where a family requests a change of tutor after at least three sessions, TutorMunk may reassign the student. This is not a breach of this agreement.

## Termination

### By the Tutor

The tutor may end this agreement by giving 2 weeks written notice by email to hello@tutormunk.com.au, and must complete scheduled sessions during the notice period unless agreed otherwise in writing.

### By TutorMunk

TutorMunk may end this agreement immediately and without notice for:

- any breach of the Code of Conduct;
- two tutor no-shows;
- expiry, suspension or cancellation of the Working with Children Check, driver licence or Australian Business Number;
- any child safety concern;
- dishonesty or material misrepresentation;
- a pattern of late, incomplete or missing session notes;
- conduct that damages the reputation of TutorMunk or its families.

### Effect of Termination

On termination the tutor must stop all sessions immediately, return or destroy all TutorMunk materials, and delete all family addresses, contact details and student records in their possession. Invoices for sessions properly delivered before termination remain payable. The clauses covering confidentiality, intellectual property, non-solicitation, privacy and indemnity survive termination.

## Confidentiality

The tutor must keep confidential all information about students, families, other tutors, rates, and the operations of TutorMunk. This obligation continues after termination. Family addresses are treated as strictly confidential and must never be shared or retained after an engagement ends.

## Intellectual Property

Materials created by TutorMunk are owned by TutorMunk. The tutor is given a limited licence to use them to deliver sessions during the engagement. Session notes and reports created for TutorMunk students are owned by TutorMunk. Materials created jointly are jointly owned. Materials the tutor brings from their own pre-existing work remain owned by the tutor, and the tutor grants TutorMunk a non-exclusive licence to use them for the relevant sessions. The tutor is responsible for ensuring any third party material they use is properly licensed under the Copyright Act 1968.

## Non-Solicitation

During the engagement and for 12 months after it ends, the tutor must not directly or indirectly solicit, approach or accept tutoring work from any TutorMunk student or family, whether paid or unpaid, and whether on their own account or for another business. This protects the child safety arrangements TutorMunk has in place, which do not apply to a private arrangement, and protects the relationships TutorMunk has invested in building.

## Privacy

The tutor must handle all personal information about students and families in accordance with the TutorMunk Privacy Policy and the Australian Privacy Principles. The tutor must not store family addresses or student information on personal devices beyond what is needed to deliver the session, must not photograph student work without written consent, and must not disclose any of it to a third party.

## Liability and Indemnity

The tutor indemnifies TutorMunk against any loss, damage or cost, including legal costs, arising from the tutor's breach of this agreement or the Code of Conduct, their negligence or wilful misconduct, or their infringement of a third party's intellectual property. Subject to the Australian Consumer Law, TutorMunk's total liability to the tutor is limited to the fees paid to the tutor in the 12 months before the event giving rise to the liability. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss.

## Force Majeure

Neither party is liable for a failure or delay caused by an event beyond their reasonable control, including natural disaster, extreme weather, pandemic or the action of a government authority.
