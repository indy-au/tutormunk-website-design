## Purpose of This Statement

This statement is a short, plain English summary of what personal information TutorMunk collects, why we collect it, where it is kept and who can see it. It sits alongside our full Privacy Policy. Where this summary and the Privacy Policy differ, the Privacy Policy applies.

If you do not want to provide the information described here, we may not be able to enrol your child or deliver sessions. We cannot send a tutor to a home without an address, and we cannot engage a tutor without verifying their Working with Children Check.

## What We Collect and Why

### Parents and Guardians

| What we collect | Why we collect it |
|---|---|
| Name, email, phone | To contact you about your child's sessions |
| Residential address and access details | So the tutor can attend the session safely and on time |
| Other authorised adults | So we know who may be present at a session or collect a student from the centre |
| Payment records | To invoice you and keep the financial records the law requires |
| Marketing consent | So we only send marketing to people who asked for it |

### Students

| What we collect | Why we collect it |
|---|---|
| Name, year level, school | To match the student with the right tutor and the right stage |
| Subjects and learning goals | To plan the sessions |
| Learning or health considerations you choose to share | So sessions are delivered safely and appropriately |
| Session notes and progress summaries | So you can see what was taught and what to practise |
| Attendance and the signed session record | To confirm the session took place and that an adult was present |

We do not record video or audio of any session, and students do not have accounts or logins.

### Tutors and Tutor Applicants

| What we collect | Why we collect it |
|---|---|
| Name, date of birth, contact details, address | To identify and contact the tutor |
| Working with Children Check number, state and expiry | A legal requirement before anyone works with children |
| Australian driver licence | Tutors travel to family homes |
| Qualifications, experience, resume, referees | To assess suitability to teach a subject and stage |
| ABN and bank details | To pay invoices |
| Performance and incident records | To manage the engagement and keep children safe |

### Website Visitors

| What we collect | Why we collect it |
|---|---|
| IP address, browser, pages visited | To understand how the site is used |
| Contact form and request a call details | To respond to your enquiry |

## Where Your Information Is Kept

We keep personal information in Australia wherever practicable. Some of the software we use for email, website hosting and record keeping may store information overseas. Before information goes to an overseas provider we take reasonable steps to check that it will be handled consistently with the Australian Privacy Principles.

Paper records, including signed session sheets, are collected from tutors and stored securely. Tutors must not keep copies of family addresses or student records once an engagement ends.

## Who Can See Your Information

| Who | What they can see | Why |
|---|---|---|
| TutorMunk staff | Enrolment, session, tutor and financial records | To run the service and keep children safe |
| The assigned tutor | Student first name, year level, subjects, session time, and the home address where the session takes place | To deliver the session |
| Parent or guardian | Their own enrolment and their child's session notes and reports | To stay informed about their child |
| Authorities | Information required under law, including child protection authorities and the NSW Police Force | To meet our legal and child safety obligations |

We do not sell personal information.

## Session Oversight

For in-home sessions, a responsible adult must be present in the home for the whole session and signs the session record at the end. At our centre, sessions take place in rooms visible from outside or with the door open, and staff may observe at any time. No session is recorded.

## Marketing

We only send marketing to people who have ticked the consent box. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any marketing email, or by emailing hello@tutormunk.com.au. Unsubscribing does not stop messages about your child's active enrolment.

## Your Rights

- Ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
- Ask us to correct information that is wrong or out of date.
- Ask us to delete information we no longer need, subject to the retention periods in our Privacy Policy.
- Opt out of marketing at any time.
- Make a complaint if you think your privacy has been breached.

Write to hello@tutormunk.com.au to exercise any of these rights. We respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied you may contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

## How Long We Keep It

Enrolment and session records are kept for 7 years from the end of the enrolment. Financial records are kept for 7 years because tax law requires it. Tutor records are kept for 7 years from the end of the engagement. Session notes and reports are kept for 3 years. Records relating to child safety are kept for 45 years. Enquiry and marketing records are kept for 3 years from the last contact.
