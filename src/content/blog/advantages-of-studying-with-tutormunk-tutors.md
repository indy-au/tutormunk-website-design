## Why is it better to start private lessons during childhood?

There are many reasons why a child might require the services of a private tutor. Perhaps he is struggling in school, or doing very well and easily bored in class. In other cases, the student's parents do not have the time to help with math or homework.

A tutor, whether [English](/topics/primary-english) or [Math](/topics/primary-maths), gives students the individualised attention they don't get in a crowded classroom. This benefits kids who have a hard time keeping up, as well as those who find themselves demotivated. Each lesson makes the student progress in an accelerated way.

Every student can benefit from individual tutoring , especially those who are in elementary school. Private classes also offer a safe space to speak English or practice math facts. This is comfortable for children who feel shy in front of their peers. In addition, the guardianship is flexible in terms of schedules and times.

Tutoring programs can help children and youth develop study and learning skills that will prepare them for lifelong success. Additionally, they strengthen the understanding of a topic and increase the student's confidence.

Children develop critical thinking and rekindle their passion for education. This gives them the opportunity to apply for the best universities, professions and jobs.

## What are the benefits of private tutors?

A proper mentorship can provide valuable assistance and guidance to students of all levels. It is a method that offers systematic and structured learning experiences adapted to the individual needs of each student. The advantages of private classes are too many to ignore. These include:

- Better school grades, this is the most frequent reason parents turn to tutoring. With this they seek to help their children improve their academic performance. The goal of each lesson is to help students reach their full potential through constant motivation.

- It prepares them for college. Those about to enter college will learn how to create study plans and develop study skills. Young people who attend private classes exhibit superior time management skills.

- The student receives individual and specialised attention. This is the most valued advantage by parents. A classroom can have as many as 20 and 30 students per teacher. With that many children, it's easy to overlook the special needs of those with learning difficulties. When it comes to education, nothing beats a personal and long-term approach.

- Students learn to enjoy studying. Group classes have the disadvantage that they apply several learning styles at the same time. This means that many children do not feel involved during the class. Tutoring programs are designed so that students enjoy their classes to the fullest. If the student feels comfortable, it will be reflected in her grades.

## How to choose a good educator in Sydney, Australia?

Each learning objective requires teachers with different levels of preparation. An educator for university mathematics has different training than one of English, or ESO mathematics.

Therefore, the level of preparation, previous experience, diplomas and certifications must be taken into account. Other factors to take into account are the frequency and timing of each lesson. Additionally, the teacher's experience with students the same age as your child is important.
