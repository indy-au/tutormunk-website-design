Parents often consider one to one tutoring an expensive investment they can't afford. But it is proven that one to one tuition produces the best results and is most effective.

However, it may be more affordable or maybe not as available as parents believe. Can you make use of it? Should you make use of it? Read this article on A Closer Look at [One to One English](/topics/primary-english) and [Math Tutoring](/topics/primary-maths) Benefits to get the answers.

### Why choose one to one tutoring for English and Math?

One-to-one sessions mean that you can focus on your specific needs as an individual student. Our teachers will be able to adapt their lessons according to your needs and interests so that they suit your level of knowledge, your learning style and even your personality. This means that you will learn much faster than if you were in a large group class where everyone has different levels of knowledge and experience.

One to one English and Math tutoring is ideal for students who want to improve their skills in both subjects. These types of tutoring services are also good for students who need extra help to prepare for an exam or test.

### One on One Tutoring is individualised.

One on one tutoring is individualised. A tutor will work with you to help you understand the material and learn new skills.

One on one tutoring provides a personal touch that cannot be found in group settings. This type of tutoring allows the tutor to get to know each student's strengths and weaknesses so that he or she can tailor his or her lessons accordingly.

Private tutors are always available when needed but they do not interfere with school activities or schedule changes like teachers often do.

### Feedback is personal and specific.

A tutor will be able to give you feedback on how well you've grasped the material and how best you can use this knowledge. This means that they'll be able to tailor their teaching style accordingly, making sure that they only teach things that are relevant.

Feedback is immediate. If you have questions about something during class or at home, it can be difficult for a teacher or parent to provide answers quickly enough for them to be helpful. With one to one English and Math tutoring, there's no time wasted waiting for an answer, you can ask questions immediately and receive feedback straightaway.

### There is the ability for students to get their questions answered in a timely manner.

There is the ability for students to get their questions answered in a timely manner. This is beneficial because they will not have to wait until the next day or week to receive feedback on their work. The tutor can give them feedback immediately, so they can see what they need to do better and improve their skills quickly.

Another benefit of one-on-one tutoring is that it allows students to focus on one subject at a time. This helps them develop stronger skills in each subject, because they are learning more about the topics that interest them most. It also allows them to learn at their own pace, instead of being forced into a group setting where everyone learns at different speeds and levels of understanding.

### One on one tutoring can be more productive than group tutoring.

One on one tutoring can be more productive than group tutoring, especially with the introduction of technology in the classroom.

By having a private tutor for math and English, students become more motivated to learn and achieve their goals faster. This is because they have someone who is invested in their success and will help them reach it.

Private tutoring allows students to work at their own pace compared to when they are in a classroom full of other students. The extra attention helps students understand concepts better, which results in better grades.

A private tutor can also help students develop important skills that they may not learn in school, such as time management or organisation skills. These skills are essential for succeeding in life after high school graduation.

One-on-one tutoring is more of a personal experience. Many private teachers will know the strength and weaknesses of their students and go beyond the curriculum to assist them.
