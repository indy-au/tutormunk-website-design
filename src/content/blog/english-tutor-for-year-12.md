### Why Year 12 English is Challenging

Year 12 English is not just about writing essays; it’s about mastering a range of skills, including:

-
**Textual Analysis**: Ability to analyse topics, figures and methods in detailed texts.

-
**Essay Writing**: Developing the composing of logic-based harmonious and coherent arguments properly and effectively.

-
**Creative Writing**: Generating new concepts while writing in an appropriate language.

-
**Exam Preparation**: Organizational skills and completion of all areas of the HSC English exam.

All these challenges may at times be very demanding and even almost impossible without adequate assistance. That is why an English teacher for Year 12, is needed as a guide who is equipped with the necessary tools and techniques.
