### Why English is important:

English is the language of the world. English language is one of the most popular languages in the world. There are more than 1 billion native speakers of English, and many people also speak it as a second language, it is one of the official languages of nearly every country in the world. In fact, there are only four countries where English isn't an official language: China, North Korea, Myanmar, and Tajikistan.

As the world becomes increasingly globalised, and as more countries are opening up to the idea of sending their children to study abroad, it is becoming more important for young children to be able to communicate in English.

The ability to speak a second language can be essential when applying for jobs or internships abroad. Additionally, there is evidence that learning a second language at an early age improves cognitive development in children.

It's also important that your child listens to music in the English language. The more they hear it, the faster they'll pick up on the words and phrases used in it.

### Teach them about etiquette and manners using English words.

Teaching your child about manners and etiquette is one of the best things you can do for them. It helps them become more confident in social situations, it teaches them that there are rules in life, and it helps them know how to treat people with respect.

There are many ways you can teach your child about etiquette and manners using English words. Here are some ideas:

- Use words like "please" and "thank you" every day when you ask them to do something (or after they've done something)

- Say thank you when someone does something for you (like if someone opens the door for you)

- Use words like "excuse me" when you need to get past someone or ask them something directly (instead of walking into their personal space)
