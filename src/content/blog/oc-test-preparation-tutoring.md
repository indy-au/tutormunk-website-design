### What Is the OC Test?

The OC test assesses a student’s abilities in English, mathematics, and general ability. These tests require critical thinking, problem-solving skills, and the ability to work under time constraints. For many students, it can be their first major academic challenge, and excelling in it opens doors to advanced educational opportunities.
