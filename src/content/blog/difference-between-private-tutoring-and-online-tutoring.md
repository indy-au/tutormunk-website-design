Private tutoring is not the same as online tutoring, and for many students, private tutoring offers a much better overall experience. Online tutoring, or home tutoring, is generally provided from any location, whether from your home or the tutor’s home. This can be quite convenient and is in-demand among rural students who may not have access to a professional tutor where they live.

Private tutoring refers to the one-to-one learning experience between an educator and a student, or a group of students.

Private tutoring is a one-on-one, in-person learning experience. The benefit of private tutoring is that all learning is carried out on a one-to-one basis. This enables the tutor to help you achieve your individual goals and improve your knowledge and understanding of the subject at hand.

Online tutoring, on the other hand, takes place over the internet. Online tutoring allows you to get help from a tutor who may not be local to you, but who can still offer valuable advice and guidance on your coursework.
