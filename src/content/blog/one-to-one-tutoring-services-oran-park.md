### 1. Make sure that you choose a highly experienced tutor who has taught in the past
If you want your child to get the best out of this experience then make sure that you choose a highly experienced one to one tutor who has taught in the past and had great results with their students. This way you can be assured that they will know what they are doing and will be able to teach your child effectively.

### 2. Check if they have any references or testimonials from previous customers
If someone is offering private tutoring services then it is always a good idea to ask for references or testimonials from previous customers so that you can see what other people think about them and whether or not they were happy with their service.
