### How tutors assist the student with their homework

The tutors are not assistants whom learners ask for help, but academic mentors who equip students to succeed. Here’s how they help:

-
Clarifying Doubts: Tutors explain concepts that the students may find difficulty in understanding in a simple manner to enable the students to master the content.

-
Building Study Skills: They explain how to study time effectively and how to solve certain types of exercises so that homework can be more easily approached and dealt with.

-
Fostering Independence: Homework is encouraged to be done independently with tutors gradually pulling back from helping the child solving the assignments.

-
Personalised Attention: Compared to conventional learning environments, tutors should only deal with the learner’s requirements, addressing them directly.

-
Time Management: They assist students to understand the to do list, how to sequence their activities, and achieve set goals and objectives without undue pressure.
