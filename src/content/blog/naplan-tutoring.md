## What is Naplan?

Naplan is a national assessment program for all students in years 3, 5 and 7. The assessments are administered by the Australian Curriculum, Assessment and Reporting Authority (ACARA). The main purpose of Naplan is to provide information about student learning, to help teachers improve the quality of their teaching and to inform parents about their child’s progress.

The assessments are conducted over two weeks in May each year. One week involves testing for reading, spelling and grammar and punctuation (RSP), while the other week involves testing for writing (W) and numeracy (N).
