When it comes to helping students achieve academic success, not all tutoring methods are created equal. While group classes can offer a general learning experience, more and more parents and students in Sydney are turning to one-to-one tutoring for its effectiveness. At TutorMunk, we specialize in personalised, one-on-one tutoring that is designed to meet each student’s individual learning needs. Here's why one-to-one tutoring consistently delivers better results than group settings.

### Personalised Attention

In a group class, a tutor may be managing five to ten students at once, making it difficult to give each child the attention they need. In contrast, one-to-one tutoring at TutorMunk allows the tutor to focus entirely on one student. This level of attention helps identify the student’s strengths, weaknesses, and learning style, allowing for a truly personalised approach.

### Customised Learning Plans

Every child learns at a different pace. In a classroom or group tutoring setting, the lesson moves on regardless of whether every student fully understands the topic. With one-on-one tutoring, the pace and content of each session are customised. At TutorMunk, our tutors create a unique learning plan tailored to the individual needs of the student, ensuring that no concept is left unclear and no question goes unanswered.

### Stronger Student-Tutor Relationship

A close working relationship between a tutor and student helps build trust and boosts a student’s confidence. In a one-to-one setting, students are more likely to engage, ask questions, and express difficulties they may be too shy to mention in a group. Our tutors at TutorMunk develop a comfortable learning environment where students feel supported and encouraged.

### Targeted Support for School Curriculum

Our one-on-one tutoring sessions are aligned with the Australian school curriculum. Whether a student is in primary or high school, preparing for NAPLAN, selective school exams, or simply needing support in Maths and English, TutorMunk tutors provide focused guidance that directly supports school progress.

### Flexible Scheduling and Convenience

TutorMunk understands that every family has different routines. With our personalised tutoring service, we offer flexible scheduling that works around the student’s school hours and extracurricular activities. Sessions can be held at our [Gregory Hills](/tutoring-in-gregory-hills) centre or in your own home, offering convenience without compromising quality.

### Faster Progress and Better Academic Results

With focused attention and customised support, students in one-to-one tutoring tend to show faster academic improvement. They develop better study habits, perform well in tests, and gain the confidence needed to succeed in school. Many parents at TutorMunk have seen noticeable improvement in their child’s grades within just a few weeks of starting one-to-one sessions.

### Conclusion

While group classes have their place, one-to-one tutoring offers a clear advantage when it comes to personalised learning and academic growth.
