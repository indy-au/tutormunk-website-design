## What are the benefits of studying English as a child?

As experts explain, children learning a second (or even a third) language in childhood will help them in many aspects of their lives. Some of the main advantages are:

### FASTER AND EASIER LEARNING

Young children do not have to make an effort to learn, they do it innately and they acquire knowledge at the same time as their brain structure develops; as age advances, the acquisition of knowledge is slower and more difficult. For this reason, the sooner they start studying English as children , the easier it will be for them.

### BETTER PERFORMANCE IN STUDIES

Children who learn English early on have been shown to develop other skills that help them later in their academic performance:

- They have more creative capacity, problem solving and mental agility.

- Easier to pay attention and memorise.

- Increase your communication skills.

- Greater ease when learning a third language.

- Efficiency in carrying out several tasks at the same time.

### WORLD'S GREATEST KNOWLEDGE

Learning English from childhood will make the little ones know another culture, they will have a more open mind, knowing different points of view and ways of life. In addition, they will be able to communicate with people of other nationalities and will have greater access to the world.

### GOODBYE SHAME!

One of the best characteristics of children when they are very young is that they are not ashamed of anything, so they will not be afraid of making mistakes when pronouncing and speaking this language. Thus, when they grow up and shame comes, they will have the confidence and security to pronounce English correctly and they will have no problem speaking it.

### MORE CHANCES OF GETTING A JOB

Currently, the fact of being bilingual or having a very good command of English is one of the skills that companies value the most when looking for a candidate to work in their business. Therefore, studying English from a young age will open many doors for them in their professional future.

## How can we get our children to learn English?

When we teach this language to young children, we must take into account that the fundamental thing is that the child accepts English in a natural way, not as a class. Therefore we can do the following:

- It is recommended that we
[adapt any game we make to English](/topics/primary-english)
. This will depend on the age of the child, up to 3 years of age we can introduce English without using Spanish, but, when they are older than this age and have never been taught English, learning is more complicated, since they have very good Spanish. settled in his brain. In the latter case it is better to use simultaneous translation in complicated sentences, repeating the same thing several times. There are many games to learn English for children , it is best to try and find those that amuse and entertain them.

- Encourage the reading of stories in English. One of the best ways to teach children to read in English is through bilingual stories with pictures and short sentences in this language.

- Currently, there are also books with audio in English. In this way, the child will use two skills at the same time: visual and auditory.

- We must convey to them that learning English or any other language can be fun . Our attitude is fundamental, it must be positive at all times, so that the child takes it with enthusiasm.

- You have to put their favourite movies in English . Currently, almost all DVDs offer the opportunity to change the language. In addition, there are many videos to learn English for children for free on the Internet.

- Get them to listen to children's songs in English . Music has a much greater mnemonic power than sight and also allows repetition out loud. For this reason, we can find many songs on the Internet to learn English for children that will make them have fun and learn at the same time.

- Even if we don't have a high level of English, we should try to speak to them in this language naturally , as we would in Spanish: with short and simple phrases, such as: “Come here”, “Well done!”, Where are your toys? ”
