*A blog about the common mistakes students make in school and how you can avoid them.*

There are certain things in life you can't afford to get wrong. This is particularly true when it comes to school and college work.

If you make mistakes because of carelessness or neglect while completing your school studies, in many cases this can mean failing courses, receiving mediocre grades, and in some cases, withdrawing from a course entirely.

## 1. Not asking questions when something is unclear.

It's important for students to understand the material, but it's also important for them to ask questions when they don't understand something. If a teacher doesn't answer your question, don't think that you won't be able to solve the problem on your own.

## 2. Not taking notes or rewriting notes after class.

Taking notes during class will help you remember what was discussed in lecture, allowing you to focus on what the teacher is saying and not having to worry about writing down notes at the same time. You can then rewrite your notes after each class so that they are more organised and easier to understand later on down-the-road when you need them most!

## 3. Not using resources available at school (e.g., tutors).

There are so many resources available at school that can help make learning easier! Tutors are available through the school if you need extra help or have any questions about homework assignments or exams coming up soon! If there aren't any available in person, there may be an online tutor who can help out via Skype or other similar type of platform depending on your location and needs!
