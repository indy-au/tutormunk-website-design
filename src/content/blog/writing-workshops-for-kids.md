## Why Writing Matters for Young Learners

Writing isn’t just about spelling and grammar, it’s about communication, imagination, and self-expression. Through writing, children learn to organise their thoughts, explore new ideas, and build confidence in their ability to express themselves. These skills are crucial not only for school success but also for lifelong learning and personal development.
