### Thus, the question may arise as to why Early Childhood Education is so crucial.

Young minds are tender, they do not take time to capture an idea or information provided to them by people. Education once again is important in the life of a child for it can influence him in the right manner if done at an early age.

This time social skills, language, manners and thinking abilities and physical skills of a child are being shaped. But if the social factor in education is given to them they can maximise their potential as adults. That is the kind of institution that lays a foundation of character and molds the entire person and their destiny.
