Tutors are necessary because everyone learns differently, but not everyone has access to a teacher who applies an effective teaching style.

With lessons from a qualified TutorMunk tutors, the student can improve their grades and learn English or math in a shorter period of time.

Mentoring helps to internalise what has been learned and then share the knowledge with other people. Our tutors offer a flexible and private education, taking into account the learning style of the student. Even professionals can benefit from private lessons.

### Why is it better to start private classes during childhood?

There are many reasons why a child might require the services of a private guardian. Perhaps he is struggling in school or is doing very well and easily bored in class. Capable is somewhat nervous and just needs help to build confidence in the lessons. In other cases, the student’s parents do not have the time to help with math or homework.

A tutor, whether English or math, gives students the individualised attention they don’t get in a crowded classroom. This benefits child who has difficulty keeping up, as well as those who are unmotivated. Each lesson makes the student progress in an accelerated way.

Every student can benefit from individual tutoring, especially those who are in elementary school. Private classes also provide a safe space to speak English or practice math facts. This is comfortable for children who are shy around their peers. In addition, guardianship is flexible in terms of hours and times.

Mentoring programs can help children and youth develop study and learning skills that will prepare them for success throughout their lives. They also strengthen understanding of a topic and increase student confidence.

The tutors provide personalised tutoring programs that will teach students the skills they need to meet the challenges of school. Children develop critical thinking and rekindle their passion for education. This gives them the opportunity to aspire to the best universities, professions, and jobs.

### What are the benefits of TutorMunk Private Tutors?

Adequate mentorship can provide valuable assistance and guidance to students at all levels. It is a method that offers systematic and structured learning experiences adapted to the individual needs of each student. The advantages of private lessons are too many to ignore. These include:

- Better school grades, this is the most frequent reason parents turn to tutor. With this, they seek to help their children improve their academic performance. The goal of each lesson is to help students reach their full potential through constant motivation.

- It prepares them for college. Those who are about to enter college will learn how to create study plans and develop study skills. Young people who attend private classes display superior time management skills.

- The student receives individual and specialised attention. This is the advantage most valued by parents. A classroom can have up to 20-30 students per teacher. With that number of children, it is easy to overlook the special needs of those with learning difficulties. When it comes to education, nothing beats a long-term, personal approach.

- Students learn to enjoy studying. Group classes have the disadvantage that they apply several learning styles at the same time. This means that many children do not feel involved during class. Tutoring programs are designed so that students enjoy their classes to the fullest. If the student feels comfortable, it will be reflected in their grades.

### How to choose a good educator?

Each learning objective requires teachers with different levels of preparation. An educator for university mathematics has a different training than one in English, or in mathematics.

Therefore, the level of preparation, previous experience, diplomas, and certifications must be taken into account. Other factors to take into account are the frequency, hours, and price of each lesson. Additionally, the teacher’s experience with students the same age as your child is important.
